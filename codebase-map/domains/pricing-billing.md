# Domain — pricing-billing

## Purpose
Compute the price of a shipment, manage the contracts that govern those prices, generate quotes, accept payments, settle invoices, and detect fraud. The "money" half of the business.

## Key flows
**Quote → contract → invoice (sketch, partial confirmation):**
1. A quote request originates (often from a frontend or a partner API) and lands in `quote-manager-backend` or `uship-quotes`.
2. Contract pricing logic in `contract-pricing-backend` consults a `Django` carrier service, an `Impersonator`, a `User Management` service, and a `Location Provider` to resolve rates. **Verified P0:** none of those REST clients have timeouts configured, and `DjangoServiceImpl.java:37` has a mathematically broken pagination formula.
3. Final price is applied by `rateengine` / ML rate models for non-contracted lanes.
4. Invoice is generated by `invoices`; `payment-backend` settles.
5. `fraud-detector` checks transactions out-of-band.
6. `autoims-backend` syncs the truth into AutoIMS; `lead-parser` ingests new lead emails into the funnel.

## Pricing-stack boundary (added 2026-05-12)

The three pricing-related services + `ml-service-dispatcher` overlap in conceptual scope. The deepening passes have surfaced enough to draw the boundary:

| Service | Owns | Doesn't own |
|---|---|---|
| `contract-pricing-backend` (Quarkus, seed) | **Per-customer / per-contract pricing**: contract rules, customer overrides, region surcharges. | Quote orchestration. ML predictions. |
| `quote-manager-backend` (Spring, seed) | **State facade**: holds the lifecycle of a quote object across the conversation; consumes/publishes quote-state Pub/Sub. | Actual rate calculation. |
| `rateengine` (Django 2.1.7, seed — **EOL flag**) | **The actual pricing engine** for non-contracted lanes. ML rate model + adjustments (multi-vehicle, enclosed, special-route). | Per-customer overrides; quote lifecycle. |
| `ml-service-dispatcher` (Python/FastAPI, seed — analytics domain) | **Synchronous gateway to ML model services**: `ml-model-rate`, `time-to-dispatch`, confidence scorers. | Business adjustments (those live in `rateengine`); quote lifecycle. |

**Flow**: `quote-manager-backend` orchestrates → `contract-pricing-backend` is consulted for per-customer terms → `rateengine` computes base rate (calling `ml-service-dispatcher` underneath for the ML predictions) → `invoices` settles → `payment-backend` collects.

**Open architectural question**: should `rateengine` (EOL Django) be rewritten as a Quarkus or Python-FastAPI service, with the ML calls going directly through `ml-service-dispatcher` rather than being wrapped by Django? That's a 1-quarter effort. The lifecycle/security argument (EOL framework) is the strongest forcing function; the ergonomic argument (the Django-wrapping-modern-Python-services pattern is awkward) is secondary.
